About IPTVNetworkHub

IPTVNetworkHub is a small, focused streaming service: one subscription that brings live television, sports, films and series together in a single guide, on the devices you already own.

Last updated 25 September 2026Approximately 4 minutes to read
In short: no dish, no box and no long-term contract. Choose a plan, install the app on a device you already have, sign in, and your guide is ready.

Most people who come to us are trying to solve the same problem: their favourite channels, sports and films are spread across several subscriptions, several apps and several bills. We built IPTVNetworkHub to put that back into one place — a single guide you can browse, on whichever screen is in front of you at the time.

We are not a television network and we do not produce content. We supply access to a streaming service and the support that goes with it, so what you are really buying from us is a straightforward way to watch.

What you get

Every plan includes the full channel guide, so the only real difference between them is how long you want to watch for. What is included:

  • Live TV — sports, news, entertainment and family channels, organised into categories you can browse quickly.
  • Movies and series — an on-demand library alongside the live channels, so you can watch what you want when you want it.
  • A programme guide (EPG) — see what is on now and what is coming up, with start and finish times where the service provides them.
  • Quality up to 4K — available picture quality depends on the channel or title, your device and your connection.
  • One subscription across your devices — install it on the screens you use, within the simultaneous-stream limit of your plan.

You can see the current plans and prices on the pricing section of the homepage. What is on offer is always visible there rather than buried in a brochure.

What you can watch on

The service is designed to run on the devices people already own. Compatibility varies by model and operating system, and a compatible player has to exist for that platform — that is the one thing worth checking before you subscribe.

Smart TV

Compatible Smart TV platforms, straight from your living room.

Android TV Box

Turn any television into a smart screen with a compatible Android TV device.

Android

Phones and tablets running Android, for watching at home or on the move.

iPhone & iPad

Supported Apple mobile devices, wherever you happen to be.

Amazon Fire TV Stick

A quick way to get the service onto a television through the Fire TV Stick.

Windows & Mac

A full-size viewing experience on your desktop or laptop computer.

If you are not sure whether your device is supported, message us with the model and we will tell you before you pay. Our IPTV guides also walk through setup for the most common platforms.

How we support you

Setup is where most people need a hand, so support is built into the way we work rather than bolted on:

  • Step-by-step guides — written for Smart TV, Android, Android TV and Firestick, in plain language.
  • A real person on WhatsApp — for questions before you buy, help during setup and anything that goes wrong afterwards.
  • Setup help on your device — tell us what you are watching on and we will point you at the right steps.

How we try to work

Three things we care about, because they are the things that frustrate people about subscription services:

  • Plain pricing. No hidden contracts and no auto-renewal you did not ask for. You pay for a term, and at the end of it you decide whether to continue.
  • You can try before you pay. New subscribers get a one-day free trial so you can test the service on your own device first.
  • Talk to a human. Ask before you buy. We would rather answer a question up front than have you work it out afterwards.
