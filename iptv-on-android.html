Android setup guide

IPTV on Android

Use IPTV on an Android phone, tablet or Android TV device with a player that supports your provider's authorized login or playlist format.

Updated September 2026Approximately 10 minutes
Before you begin: install apps from Google Play or another source you trust, and use IPTV services and content you are authorized to access.

Android supports a wide range of IPTV players. The same basic process applies on a phone, tablet and Android TV, although remote-control navigation and app layouts may differ.

What you need

  • An Android phone, tablet or Android TV device with current system updates.
  • A stable internet connection and enough storage for the player app.
  • A compatible player installed from Google Play or a legitimate publisher source.
  • Your provider's username, password, server URL, M3U playlist or portal details.

Set up IPTV on Android

  1. Open Google Play and search for a compatible IPTV player.
  2. Install the app, review its permissions and open it.
  3. Select the login method your provider supports, such as an account or playlist.
  4. Enter the supplied details exactly, then let the app load categories and programme data.
  5. Open Live TV, Movies or Series and test playback on a few channels.

Phone and tablet tips

Use a stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection and enable automatic app updates. Landscape mode can make the guide easier to scan, while casting to a supported display can give you a larger screen.

Android TV tips

Keep the player and Android TV system updated, use the remote-friendly version of the app and make sure the device has free storage. Ethernet is useful when the device is close to your router.

Troubleshooting

Refresh the playlist, confirm the credentials and test another network or channel. If the app crashes, clear its cache and restart the device. Persistent account or server errors should be checked with your provider.
