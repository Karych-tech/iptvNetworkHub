Categories

Explore IPTV Channels by Category

Channels are grouped so you can move straight to the kind of programming you want instead of scrolling through one long list.

Sports Channels

Sports categories cover football, basketball and other live events. The lineup includes channels such as ESPN, NBA, beIN Sports, Bundesliga, Champions League, Jupiler Pro League, LaLiga, Premier League, RMC Sport and Ziggo Sport. Competition schedules and what is shown on any given day are set by the broadcasters, not by IPTVNetworkHub.

News Channels

News categories are for viewers who want rolling coverage and current-affairs programming. Channels such as 6M and FOX sit alongside other news entries in the guide, so you can move between headlines and analysis without changing apps.

Entertainment Channels

Entertainment covers general-interest programming, lifestyle shows and streaming-brand channels. The lineup includes Netflix, Hulu, HBO Max, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV, Fubo TV, Canal+ and OCS, each shown with its own artwork so the guide is easy to scan.

Movies & Series

Movies and series entries are grouped separately from live television, so you can switch between a live channel and on-demand titles. The Film channel artwork appears in this category alongside the on-demand movies and series rows shown on the home page.

Kids & Family Channels

Kids and family programming is kept in its own category so it is quick to reach and easy to keep apart from everything else. Disney Channel appears here, and the guide keeps family viewing in one place rather than mixed into general entertainment.

International Channels

International entries bring regional and documentary programming into the same guide. Planete+ appears in this category. Which channels a viewer sees can vary by region, so the lineup shown here is an overview of the categories rather than a fixed list.
