Questions

Good to know before you start.

What do I need to get started?

Just an internet connection and one supported device — smart TV, phone, streaming box, or browser. Install the app, sign in, and your guide loads automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. Pause or cancel your subscription from your account at any time.

How many devices can stream at once?

That depends on your plan — from one simultaneous stream on Basic up to five on Premium. You can install the app on unlimited devices; the limit is how many can stream at the same time.

Do you offer a free trial?

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on any plan. No commitment required, and you can switch plans or cancel before it ends.
