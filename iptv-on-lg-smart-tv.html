LG Smart TV setup guide

How to Set Up IPTV on LG Smart TV

LG Smart TVs run webOS, with apps installed from the LG Content Store. This guide covers checking your webOS version, installing a compatible IPTV player, connecting your authorized provider account and getting the programme guide to appear.

Updated September 17, 2026Approximately 15 minutes
Before you begin: IPTV is a technology for delivering television over an internet connection. Use IPTV services and content you are authorized to access and that comply with applicable laws and provider terms.

IPTV on an LG Smart TV works the same way as on any other platform: you install a player app, and the service you are authorized to use supplies the account, channel list and guide data. What differs on LG is where the app comes from, how the Home dashboard is organised and the fact that the Magic Remote makes typing provider details unusually quick.

What You Need to Set Up IPTV on an LG Smart TV

  • An LG Smart TV running webOS 3.0 or newer, with webOS 6 or a recent version preferred.
  • A stable internet connection, preferably Ethernet or a strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi network.
  • An LG account signed in on the TV, if the set asks for one when installing apps.
  • A compatible IPTV player available for your webOS version and country.
  • Login details from a provider you are authorized to use: a username, password and server or portal address, or an M3U playlist link.

How the LG Smart TV IPTV Setup Process Works

  1. Check the TV model and webOS version, then install pending software updates.
  2. Connect the TV to the internet and confirm it is online.
  3. Open the LG Content Store and install a compatible IPTV player.
  4. Open the player and choose the login method your provider supports.
  5. Enter your provider details exactly as supplied.
  6. Refresh the playlist and EPG, then open Live TV or the on-demand library.

Step 1 - Check Your LG TV Model and webOS Version

Press the Settings button on the remote, open All Settings, then General and About This TV. Note the model and the webOS version. If a software update is offered, install it and allow the TV to restart; the webOS version, not the screen size, decides which player builds your TV can run.

Step 2 - Connect the TV to the Internet

In All Settings, open Connection and then Network or Wi-Fi Connection. A wired Ethernet link is the most stable option if the router is nearby. Otherwise connect to the 5 GHz band where available, and use the TV's connection test to confirm the network is reachable before you download anything.

Step 3 - Install a Compatible IPTV Player

Press the Home button and open the LG Content Store. Search for an IPTV player, check the developer and the supported models, then choose Install. When the install completes, launch the app from the Home dashboard launcher.

If nothing suitable appears, try different search terms, confirm the TV software is current and check whether the player supports your webOS version. Availability also differs by country, so a player listed on one LG TV may not appear on another.

Step 4 - Choose the Login Method

Players typically offer an Xtream Codes API login, an M3U playlist URL or a portal or MAC-based login. Choose the method your provider supports. Entering credentials in a different format than the one issued is the most common cause of a login that looks correct but fails.

Step 5 - Enter Your IPTV Login Details

Enter your IPTV Network Hub credentials exactly as provided. An Xtream Codes-style login normally needs a username, a password and a server or portal URL. With a Magic Remote you can point at the on-screen keyboard and click each key, which is considerably quicker than pressing arrow buttons; check letters and symbols as you go, since a single wrong character stops the login.

Do not invent a server address or reuse credentials from a different service. Once you submit the form, allow the player time to download the channel categories and on-demand library.

Step 6 - Load the EPG and Start Watching

Open Live TV, choose a category and select a channel. If the programme guide is empty or shifted by an hour, use the player's EPG option with the details supplied by your provider, and check that the TV's time zone and clock are set automatically. An incorrect time zone is a common and easily missed cause of a guide that looks wrong.

Improve Playback on an LG TV

Close other apps so the TV's memory is free for the player, keep webOS and the player updated, and enable any power-saving or quick-start options that suit your viewing. Where the router is close to the TV, a wired connection is worth the cable. Test a second channel when one stutters: one failing channel points at that stream, while everything failing points at the network or the service.

Troubleshooting LG Smart TV IPTV Issues

  • App will not install: confirm the TV is online, has free storage, is signed in where required and is running current software.
  • App is not in the store: check the player's supported webOS versions, or use an external device such as a Fire TV Stick, Android TV box or Google TV device.
  • Login rejected: recheck the username, password and server address, and confirm the login method matches the one your provider issued.
  • Channels will not load: refresh the playlist, confirm the service is online and contact your provider if the account or server is unavailable.
  • Guide is empty or an hour out: refresh the EPG, verify the configuration supplied by your provider and correct the TV's time zone.
  • Frequent buffering: restart the router and TV, reduce other network activity, lower the resolution and test another channel.
  • App crashes or stutters: close background apps, clear the app's cache or reinstall it, then power the TV off at the wall rather than leaving it in standby.

When No Player Is Available for Your Model

Early webOS and pre-webOS LG televisions have far fewer player options, and some have none at all. Rather than replacing the TV, an inexpensive streaming device turns it into a fully capable IPTV screen. Our Firestick guide and Android guide cover those setups step by step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install IPTV on an LG Smart TV?

Yes, if a compatible player is available in the LG Content Store for your model and webOS version. Install the player, then sign in with details from a service you are authorized to use.

Which LG TVs support IPTV players?

webOS 3.0 and newer sets have the widest choice, and recent versions are best supported. Very old netcast or early webOS models may have no suitable player.

Is there an official IPTV app for LG Smart TV?

There is no single official IPTV app. Several compatible players are published for LG webOS TVs, and availability varies by country and model year.

How do I install an app on an LG Smart TV?

Press Home, open the LG Content Store, search for the app and select Install. It then appears in the Home dashboard launcher.

How do I enter IPTV login details on an LG TV?

Open the player, choose its provider login method and enter the supplied username, password and server or portal address with the Magic Remote or on-screen keyboard.

How do I add the EPG on an LG webOS TV?

Guide data often loads with the playlist. If it is empty or an hour out, use the player's EPG option with your provider's details and check the TV's time zone.

Why does my LG TV IPTV app keep buffering?

Check the connection, restart the router and TV, reduce other network activity, update the player and test another channel before reinstalling anything.

Can I use IPTV on an LG TV without a set-top box?

Often yes, when a compatible player exists for your webOS version. Otherwise a supported external device is the practical alternative.
